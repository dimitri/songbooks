Just you

Rehearsal to gig

Your band

One shared chart per song — any member fixes a typo or changes a key, and everyone sees it before the next rehearsal. Setlists, gig notes, and a click track built for the stage, not bolted on after.

Beyond one band

Your community

Run a jam night, a worship team, or a teaching studio. Roles decide who can edit the repertoire, manage events, or just read — invite links carry the right role automatically, so onboarding is one tap.

Everything lives in one place

Not a chart viewer with a setlist bolted on — one app, built in that order.

Shared, not forwarded

Every member works from the same variant of a song — fork it to make your own version, don't duplicate it to edit in secret.

Click track, built in

A metronome that already knows the song's tempo and time signature, with your display preferences remembered across every song in the set.

Edit in place, get it right

A chart editor built into the page itself — type a chord, add a section, drop in a riff — backed by a plain text format that renders the same polished chart on screen and in the PDF, with no separate typesetting step.

PDF for the stand

Export a single chart, a full setlist, or a whole songbook — with each song's tempo and time signature printed where a player actually looks for it.

The original recording, right there

Link the YouTube video or an mp3 extract to a song and play it alongside the chart — scrub to a section, drop a marker, practice against the real thing.
