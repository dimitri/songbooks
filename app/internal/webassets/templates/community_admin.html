Quick add (personal invite)

For someone who has no account yet: they're on the roster straight away, and the personal link you get lets them finish their account.

Add an existing account

The username they log in with.

Group invite link

Type the names you expect, then get one link to share — on WhatsApp, say. Each person who opens it picks their name, then creates an account or logs in, and joins with the role below. You can change anyone's role afterwards in the members table.

Community link or QR code

One open link, no names needed: anyone who follows it creates an account (or logs in) and joins straight away with the role on the link. Make one link per role rather than promoting people afterwards, so you never hand out a more powerful link by mistake. A "guest" link only lets people read — handy for a QR code on the door at a jam.
