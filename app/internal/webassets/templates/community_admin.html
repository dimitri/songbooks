Group invite link

Type the names you expect, then get ONE link to share — on WhatsApp, say. Each person who opens it picks their own name (or "someone else"), then creates an account or logs in, joining at the role picked below; reassign afterwards via the members table above if needed.

Community link / QR code

One open link (and QR code), no names needed — anyone who follows it creates an account (or logs into one they already have) and joins immediately, holding whichever role you pick here. Mint a separate link per role rather than share one and promote people after, so you never hand out a more-privileged link by mistake. A "guest" link is the read-only role meant for an open/anonymous-feeling signup, e.g. a jam event's door QR code.
