Songbook or course

A course groups its songs in lessons, in order, each with its text and files, and shows members where they are in it. Access, sharing and downloads stay the same.

Shared or private

Shared, whoever can open a song can also copy it into their own songbook, a band’s or another community’s. Private, the songs can be opened, played and downloaded but not copied — an author’s lessons stay theirs. Either way the songbook can be sold separately, below.

Who can open this songbook

Unticked, every current member can open it. Ticked, only the members you give access to below can — for a songbook sold separately (payment itself is handled outside the app for now; a purchase will give access the same way). Changes save straight away.
