Pick what your community needs

Private, members-only lessons

Video lessons gated to paying members — nothing public until you decide otherwise.

Member uploads

Let students post their own practice videos — for feedback, for a group challenge, or just to show up.

Feedback that stays scoped

A note on the exact bar or section it's about — private to one student, or shared with the group. Not an open chat room you have to police.

Charts your students edit live
